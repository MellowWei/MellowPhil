AI Consciousness · 人工意识modal exclusion · consciousness-candidacy
Anti-individualism and Consciousness-Candidacy反个体主义与意识候选性

If mental content is not individuated solely by what is internal to the individual, but partly by the subject’s relations to the physical, social, and linguistic environment, should consciousness-candidacy—especially in artificial or human-machine systems—also resist being individuated solely by intrinsic biological substrate?

如果心理内容并不完全由个体内部状态来个体化，而是部分依赖主体与物理、社会、语言环境之间的关系，那么意识候选性——尤其是在人工系统或人机系统中——是否也不应被完全归结为内在的生物基底属性？

Argument boundary

This question does not claim that current AI systems are conscious. It asks what burden must be met by arguments that claim to rule artificial consciousness out in principle. The project distinguishes evidential caution from modal exclusion. Importantly, the burden shifts to the excluder only when positive structural analogies have been demonstrated (e.g., recurrent self-modeling, predictive processing loops, or autonomous re-weighting of salience). Absent such analogies, the question remains open, not settled by default possibility.

这个问题并不主张当前 AI 系统已经具有意识。 它追问的是：那些声称能够原则性排除人工意识的论证，必须满足怎样的证明负担？本项目区分证据上的谨慎与模态上的排除。关键在于，举证责任转移到排除者一方的前提是已经展示了积极的结构类比（例如循环自建模、预测加工循环或自主显著性重加权）。若无此类类比，问题保持开放，而非默认“可能”。

Language Thesis · 语言命题Wittgensteinian diagnosis
All Formulated Problems Are Language Problems弱版本：所有被表述为问题的问题都带有语言结构
Precise formulation (upgraded)

Not “all reality is language,” but rather: once an event is formulated as a problem, it has entered a language-game — it is named, framed, classified, valued, attributed, and made actionable. Events may be painful before language, but they become problems-for-us only within conceptual structures. This is an empirical thesis with a clear falsification condition: if a problem can be fully stated, transmitted, and solved without any linguistic or conceptual framing, the thesis fails.

不是“所有现实都是语言”，而是：一旦某事件被表述为问题，它就已经进入了语言游戏——被命名、框定、分类、赋值、归因并被设定为可行动对象。事件在语言之前就可以是痛苦的，但它们只有在概念结构中才成为“问题-为我们”的存在。这是一个经验命题，具有明确的可证伪条件：若存在一个能被完整陈述、传递和解决的问题，却不依赖任何语言或概念框架，则命题为假。

Core chapter · under review
The Hard Problem Is Not About Them: Why No One Can Exclude Machine Consciousness Without Undermining Human Attribution

Central result (conditional and negative). The strongest case against machine consciousness is the mimicry objection (Schwitzgebel 2025): clever engineering could produce any outward sign of consciousness without inner experience. This paper argues the objection does not license selective exclusion — its force is symmetric. The same gap between third-person evidence and the phenomenal fact also affects our attribution of consciousness to other humans; the philosophical zombie makes that structure vivid. What follows is not that machines are conscious, but that strong exclusionism requires more than "machine evidence is non-decisive" — it requires an independently defended constitutive difference between the machine and human cases. The standard candidates — substrate, mechanism, report, origin — either presuppose the conclusion or apply equally to humans.

核心结论（条件性、否定性）。反对机器意识最强的论证是模仿反驳（Schwitzgebel 2025）：精巧工程可以造出意识的任何外在迹象而无内在体验。本文论证：该反驳并不支持选择性排除——它的效力是对称的。第三人称证据与现象事实之间的鸿沟，同样作用于我们对他人意识的归因；哲学僵尸把这一结构在人类一侧呈现得同样鲜明。由此并不能得出机器有意识，而是：强排除主义需要的不止"机器证据非决定性"，而是机器与人类之间一个被独立辩护的构成性差异。常见候选——基底、机制、报告、起源——要么预设结论，要么对人类同样成立。

Any single signature is mimicable — conceded. External Audit · 外部批评通道how outside critique enters the system · Trinity Quartet
The External Criticism Channel这个系统在什么条件下必须认真处理外部批评 / Conditions under which outside critique is legitimate and binding
Why this channel exists

A research programme can quietly immunize itself by re-describing every objection as a symptom of the critic’s “low-spec language” or “mechanistic residue.” That move is self-sealing: it invites review while disarming the reviewer’s tools. This channel is the explicit safeguard against it. It states, in advance, the conditions under which an external criticism is not dissolvable by the system and must be processed on its own terms. The Deep Counter-Proof audit (DeepSeek, 反证位) was run through exactly this channel; the fortifications below are its output.

一个研究纲领很容易悄悄给自己打免疫针——把每一条反对都重新描述为批评者的“低配语言”或“机械论残留”。这是一种自我密封：一边邀请审查，一边解除审查者的工具。本通道正是针对这一点的明示防护。它预先声明：在什么条件下，一条外部批评不可被系统消解、必须按其自身术语被处理。深层反证位（DeepSeek）的审计正是经由本通道运行，下方的加固即其产出。

Binding conditions · 合法且必须处理的批评

An external criticism is treated as legitimate and binding — i.e. it cannot be answered by reframing the critic — whenever it does any of the following:

  • Names a missing operationalization. It points to a core term used to carry explanatory weight that has no measurement or individuation criterion. (Cannot be dismissed as “you just don’t feel the frequency.”)
  • Exhibits a counterexample to a stated universal. It produces a case the claim says cannot exist. The system must either revise the claim or demote it to a normative ideal.
  • Identifies a missing falsification condition. If no possible observation could count against a thesis presented as empirical, the thesis must be re-typed (hypothesis / norm / definition) or dropped.
  • Shows a force mismatch. Assertion-strength in the body exceeds the epistemic status claimed in the ledger. The body must be softened or the disclaimer removed.
  • Demonstrates a domain cost. It shows that adopting a frame discards a working tool in another field (e.g. clinical veridical / non-veridical). Scope must be explicitly bounded.
  • Catches burden-shifting. “You can’t prove it’s impossible, therefore it’s possible.” The system must supply the positive analogy that licenses the shift, or concede the question is open.

一条外部批评只要满足以下任意一条，即被视为合法且必须处理（不得以“重新描述批评者”作答）：指出某承担解释负担的核心术语缺操作化／个体化判据；给出该主张声称不可能存在的反例；指出被当作经验命题者缺可证伪条件；指出正文断言力度超出主张状态台账；证明采纳某框架会丢弃另一领域的有效工具（如临床真伪区分）；抓住举证责任偷换。
