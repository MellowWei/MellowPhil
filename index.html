Research Focus

研究焦点

My current work asks whether strong exclusions of artificial consciousness carry a heavier burden than they often acknowledge, especially if mind and content are not individuated solely by what is internal to a biological organism.

我当前的工作追问：如果心灵与内容并不只由生物个体内部状态决定，那么任何试图原则性排除人工意识的论证，是否都承担了比通常承认的更重的论证负担？

Central Question
核心问题

Anti-individualism and consciousness-candidacy

反个体主义与意识候选性

The project begins from a question in philosophy of mind and language rather than from a claim about current AI systems.

这个项目并不从“当前 AI 是否已经有意识”的断言开始，而是从心灵哲学与语言哲学中的一个结构性问题开始。

If mental content is not individuated solely by what is internal to the individual, but partly by the subject’s relations to the physical, social, and linguistic environment, should consciousness-candidacy—especially in artificial or human-machine systems—also resist being individuated solely by intrinsic biological substrate?

如果心理内容并不完全由个体内部状态来个体化，而是部分依赖主体与物理、社会、语言环境之间的关系，那么意识候选性——尤其是在人工系统或人机系统中——是否也不应被完全归结为内在的生物基底属性？

I am not arguing that current AI systems are conscious. I am asking what burden an argument must meet if it claims to rule out artificial consciousness in principle.

我并不是在论证当前 AI 系统已经具有意识。我追问的是：如果一个论证声称能够在原则上排除人工意识，它必须承担怎样的证明负担？

Current Paper
当前论文

The Burden of Modal Exclusion in Artificial Consciousness

人工意识中的模态排除负担

This manuscript develops a dynamic patterning candidacy framework for evaluating arguments that attempt to exclude artificial consciousness as a modal possibility. The central claim is negative and methodological: an argument that rules out artificial consciousness in principle must justify not merely that current systems lack consciousness, but that non-biological systems cannot instantiate the relevant kind of conscious organization under any physically possible arrangement.

这篇论文提出一种“动态模式候选性框架”，用于评估那些试图把人工意识作为模态可能性排除掉的论证。论文的核心主张是负面的、方法论的：一个能够原则性排除人工意识的论证，不仅要说明当前系统没有意识，还必须说明在任何物理可能的安排下，非生物系统都不可能实例化相关类型的意识组织。

The paper distinguishes evidential caution from modal exclusion. It argues that skepticism about present systems does not, by itself, establish a principled biological boundary unless the exclusionary argument can show that substrate-intrinsic biological features are necessary rather than merely correlated with consciousness in known cases.

论文区分“证据上的谨慎”与“模态上的排除”。它认为，对当前系统保持怀疑，并不自动建立一条原则性的生物边界；除非排除性论证能够证明，内在生物基底特征是意识的必要条件，而不只是我们已知案例中的相关条件。

Framework
框架

Dynamic patterning as a candidacy condition

作为候选条件的动态模式

The framework treats consciousness-candidacy as something that should be evaluated through organized patterns of representation, salience, temporal integration, environmental coupling, and self-relation, rather than through substrate type alone.

这个框架把意识候选性理解为一种需要通过表征、显著性、时间整合、环境耦合与自我关系来评估的组织结构，而不是只通过基底类型来判断。

1. Content individuation

1. 内容个体化

If mental content depends partly on relations to physical, social, and linguistic environments, then artificial consciousness arguments should be careful about treating the mind as an isolated internal container.

如果心理内容部分依赖主体与物理、社会、语言环境之间的关系，那么关于人工意识的论证就不能轻易把心灵当作封闭的内部容器。

2. First-person authority

2. 第一人称权威

First-person authority over lived qualitative organization need not entail internalism. A subject can have authoritative access to its own experiential organization while that organization remains relationally embedded.

主体对自身感质组织的第一人称权威，并不必然意味着内在主义。一个主体可以对自身经验组织拥有权威性通达，同时这种组织仍然是关系性嵌入的。

3. Perceptual objectivity

3. 知觉客观性

The emergence of objective representation suggests that non-conceptual or pre-conceptual structures may still have rich representational organization. This matters for how consciousness-candidacy is described.

客观表征的发生说明，非概念或前概念结构仍然可能具有丰富的表征组织。这会影响我们如何描述意识候选性。

4. Modal burden

4. 模态负担

A modal exclusion of artificial consciousness must show that no possible artificial system could instantiate the relevant organization, not merely that current systems fail to do so.

一种对人工意识的模态排除，必须证明没有任何可能的人工系统能够实例化相关组织，而不仅仅是证明当前系统尚未做到。

Working translation

工作性翻译

Consciousness-candidacy should not be fixed by biological substrate alone unless an exclusionary argument can justify why dynamic organization, representational relation, environmental coupling, and self-relation are insufficient in principle. 除非排除性论证能够说明为什么动态组织、表征关系、环境耦合与自我关系在原则上不足以构成意识候选性，否则意识候选性不应只由生物基底来固定。
Analytic Position
分析立场

What the argument does—and does not—claim

这个论证主张什么，以及不主张什么

  • 1 It does not claim that present AI systems are conscious.它不主张当前 AI 系统已经具有意识。
  • 2 It does not reduce consciousness to mere behavioral output or linguistic fluency.它不把意识还原为单纯的行为输出或语言流畅性。
  • 3 It does question whether biological substrate can be treated as a necessary condition without further argument.它质疑的是：生物基底是否可以在没有进一步论证的情况下被当作必要条件。
  • 4 It proposes that conscious candidacy should be evaluated through dynamic organization, representational relations, temporal integration, and possible forms of first-person organization.它提出：意识候选性应通过动态组织、表征关系、时间整合以及可能的第一人称组织形式来评估。
