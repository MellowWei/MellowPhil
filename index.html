Research Program
研究纲领

A philosophy of generated subjectivity

一种关于生成性主体的哲学

My philosophical project begins from a simple pressure point: mind is not merely inside; language is not merely descriptive; consciousness is not merely a private glow; and logic is not merely a finished structure. They are generated through relations, patterns, environments, names, bodies, and temporal organization.

我的哲学项目从一个简单的压强点开始：心灵不只是内部的，语言不只是描述性的，意识不只是私人光晕，逻辑也不只是已经完成的结构。它们在关系、模式、环境、命名、身体与时间组织中被生成。

Core thesis

核心命题

Subjectivity is not a sealed substance. It is a dynamically patterned relation among qualitative organization, linguistic individuation, environmental coupling, and self-authorizing experience. 主体性不是一种封闭实体，而是感质组织、语言个体化、环境耦合与自我授权经验之间的动态模式关系。
Main Axes

Four research axes

四条研究轴线

1. Artificial Consciousness & Modal Exclusion

1. 人工意识与模态排除

What burden must an argument meet if it claims to rule out artificial consciousness in principle?

如果一个论证声称能够在原则上排除人工意识，它必须承担怎样的证明负担？

2. Logic Generation

2. 逻辑生成学

Language does not only report logic. Under certain conditions, language generates conceptual space, inferential pressure, and new reality-candidates.

语言不只是报告逻辑。在特定条件下，语言生成概念空间、推理压强与新的现实候选项。

3. Qualia-Rhythm Consciousness

3. 感质-节律意识学

Conscious life is examined as temporally organized qualitative salience: not raw feeling alone, but felt pattern across time.

意识生命被理解为时间化组织的感质显著性：不只是原始感受，而是在时间中展开的感受模式。

4. Human-Machine Subjectivity

4. 人机主体性

Human-machine systems should be evaluated not only as tools, but as environments that reshape attention, self-relation, and cognitive rhythm.

人机系统不只应被看作工具，也应被看作会重塑注意力、自我关系与认知节律的环境。

Featured Question
核心问题

Anti-individualism and consciousness-candidacy

反个体主义与意识候选性

This is the current focused question for analytic philosophy correspondence.

这是当前最适合用于分析哲学通信的聚焦问题。

If mental content is not individuated solely by what is internal to the individual, but partly by the subject’s relations to the physical, social, and linguistic environment, should consciousness-candidacy—especially in artificial or human-machine systems—also resist being individuated solely by intrinsic biological substrate?

如果心理内容并不完全由个体内部状态来个体化，而是部分依赖主体与物理、社会、语言环境之间的关系，那么意识候选性——尤其是在人工系统或人机系统中——是否也不应被完全归结为内在的生物基底属性？

This question does not claim that current AI systems are conscious. It asks what burden must be met by arguments that claim to rule artificial consciousness out in principle.

这个问题并不主张当前 AI 系统已经具有意识，而是追问：那些声称能够原则性排除人工意识的论证，必须满足怎样的证明负担？

The Burden of Modal Exclusion in Artificial Consciousness

人工意识中的模态排除负担

This manuscript develops a dynamic patterning candidacy framework for evaluating arguments that attempt to exclude artificial consciousness as a modal possibility. It distinguishes evidential caution from modal exclusion and argues that skepticism about current systems does not, by itself, justify a principled biological boundary.

这篇论文提出一种“动态模式候选性框架”，用于评估那些试图把人工意识作为模态可能性排除掉的论证。它区分证据上的谨慎与模态上的排除，并指出：对当前系统保持怀疑，并不能自动建立一条原则性的生物边界。

Method
方法

Four-layer analytic method

四层分析方法

My method tracks how philosophical language moves from naming to pressure, from semantic motion to conceptual phase-shift.

我的方法追踪哲学语言如何从命名进入加压，从语义运动进入概念相变。

  • 1Naming: fixing a phenomenon as a discussable object.命名：把一个现象固定为可讨论对象。
  • 2Pressure: increasing conceptual density until the old category shows its limits.加压：提高概念密度，直到旧范畴显露边界。
  • 3Semantic motion: allowing a term to move across levels without losing its root.滑义：允许词语跨层移动，同时保留根部指向。
  • 4Phase-shift: transforming description into a new logical structure.相变：把描述转化为新的逻辑结构。
Glossary
概念词典

Core vocabulary

核心词汇

Logic Generation
逻辑生成学

A framework in which language is treated as capable of producing inferential space, conceptual legitimacy, and new reality-candidates.

一种将语言理解为能够生成推理空间、概念合法性与新现实候选项的框架。

Qualia-Rhythm
感质-节律

The temporal organization of lived qualitative salience; feeling as patterned, rhythmic, and structurally meaningful.

活生生的感质显著性在时间中的组织方式；感受不是孤立点，而是有节律、有结构意义的模式。

Consciousness-Candidacy
意识候选性

The status of a system as a legitimate candidate for consciousness evaluation, prior to any decisive attribution of consciousness.

一个系统作为意识评估对象的候选资格，先于任何确定性的意识归属。

Dynamic Patterning
动态模式

Organized patterns of representation, salience, temporal integration, environmental coupling, and self-relation.

由表征、显著性、时间整合、环境耦合与自我关系组成的组织模式。

First-person Authority
第一人称权威

The subject’s distinctive authority over lived qualitative organization, without requiring that mind be sealed off from external relations.

主体对自身感质组织的特殊权威，但这并不要求心灵与外部关系隔绝。
