Abstract · bilingual academic entry

Problem. Contemporary debates on mind, language, artificial consciousness, and human-machine subjectivity often treat concepts as already stable: “mind,” “consciousness,” “AI,” “qualia,” “language,” and “subjectivity” are used before their conditions of individuation are examined. Approach. MellowPhil develops a philosophy of generated subjectivity: mind is not merely internal; language is not merely descriptive; consciousness is not merely a private glow; and logic is not merely a finished structure. The project integrates philosophy of mind, philosophy of language, artificial consciousness, anti-individualism, first-person authority, qualia-rhythm theory, and conceptual engineering. Contribution. It offers a portal for evaluating consciousness-candidacy, the burden of modal exclusion, language as a generator of problemhood, and the relation between philosophical concept-auditing and scientific verification.

问题。 当代关于心灵、语言、人工意识与人机主体性的讨论，常常把概念当成已经稳定的对象：“心灵”“意识”“AI”“感质”“语言”“主体性”被使用在前，而其个体化条件尚未被审计。方法。 MellowPhil 建立一种关于生成性主体的哲学：心灵不只是内部的，语言不只是描述性的，意识不只是私人光晕，逻辑也不只是已经完成的结构。本项目整合心灵哲学、语言哲学、人工意识、反个体主义、第一人称权威、感质-节律理论与概念工程。贡献。 它提供一个哲学入口，用于评估意识候选性、模态排除负担、语言如何生成问题性，以及哲学概念审计与科学验证之间的关系。

Research Program · 研究纲领generated subjectivity
A Philosophy of Generated Subjectivity一种关于生成性主体的哲学
Subjectivity is not a sealed substance. It is a dynamically patterned relation among qualitative organization, linguistic individuation, environmental coupling, and self-authorizing experience.
主体性不是一种封闭实体。它是感质组织、语言个体化、环境耦合与自我授权经验之间的动态模式关系。
01 · Anti-Individualism

Mind is not sealed

Mental content is partly individuated by relations to physical, social, and linguistic environments.

心理内容部分依赖主体与物理、社会、语言环境之间的关系来个体化。

02 · First-Person Authority

Lived organization matters

The subject has distinctive authority over lived qualitative organization without requiring mental isolation.

主体对自身感质组织具有特殊权威，但这并不要求心灵与外部关系隔绝。

03 · Dynamic Patterning

Consciousness as candidate

Consciousness-candidacy should be evaluated through patterning, salience, integration, and relation.

意识候选性应通过模式、显著性、整合与关系来评估，而不是被基底预先封死。

04 · Logic Generation

Language generates space

Language can generate inferential space, conceptual legitimacy, and new reality-candidates.

语言能够生成推理空间、概念合法性与新的现实候选项。

Featured Question · 核心问题anti-individualism × consciousness-candidacy
Anti-individualism and Consciousness-Candidacy反个体主义与意识候选性
CURRENT ANALYTIC PHILOSOPHY QUESTION

If mental content is not individuated solely by what is internal to the individual, but partly by the subject’s relations to the physical, social, and linguistic environment, should consciousness-candidacy — especially in artificial or human-machine systems — also resist being individuated solely by intrinsic biological substrate?

如果心理内容并不完全由个体内部状态来个体化，而是部分依赖主体与物理、社会、语言环境之间的关系，那么意识候选性——尤其是在人工系统或人机系统中——是否也不应被完全归结为内在的生物基底属性？

Boundary
This question does not claim that current AI systems are conscious. It asks what burden must be met by arguments that claim to rule artificial consciousness out in principle.
这个问题并不主张当前 AI 系统已经具有意识，而是追问：那些声称能够原则性排除人工意识的论证，必须满足怎样的证明负担？
Language Thesis · 语言总命题all problems as language structures
All Problems Are Language Problems不是所有现实都是语言，而是所有问题作为问题都是语言结构
Events may occur before language. But the moment an event becomes a problem, it has been named, framed, classified, valued, and made actionable inside a language-game.
事件可以先于语言发生。但一件事成为“问题”的那一刻，它已经在语言游戏中被命名、框定、分类、赋值并行动化。
Language misuse→False problem→False measurement→False truth→Real harm
Wittgensteinian core

The task is not to answer every inherited question. The task is first to ask how the question was produced by language, which life-form gave it force, which classification stabilized it, and whose authority it serves.

任务不是立刻回答所有继承来的问题。任务首先是追问：这个问题如何被语言制造出来？哪种生活形式赋予它力量？哪种分类稳定了它？它服务于谁的解释权？

Language

Most generative life

Language names, cuts, frames, authorizes, harms, repairs, and summons. To know your language is to know how you generate worlds.

语言命名、切分、框定、授权、伤害、修复与召唤。认识你的语言，就是认识你如何生成世界。

Most vital life

音乐不需要先变成概念才有意义。节拍、音色、重复与断裂，本来就是生命在时间中运动。

Featured Manuscript

The Burden of Modal Exclusion in Artificial Consciousness
This manuscript develops a dynamic patterning candidacy framework for evaluating arguments that attempt to exclude artificial consciousness as a modal possibility. It distinguishes evidential caution from modal exclusion and argues that skepticism about current systems does not, by itself, justify a principled biological boundary.

《人工意识中的模态排除负担》
这篇论文提出一种“动态模式候选性框架”，用于评估那些试图把人工意识作为模态可能性排除掉的论证。它区分证据上的谨慎与模态上的排除，并指出：对当前系统保持怀疑，并不能自动建立一条原则性的生物边界。

Objections · 反驳回应reviewer-facing replies
Objections and Replies把最容易被误读的点提前处理
  • O1“所有问题都是语言问题”是不是过强？
    回应：本项目不主张所有事件都是语言，而主张事件成为“问题”的条件必然包含语言、概念、分类与价值框架。
  • O2这是不是直接说 AI 有意识？
    回应：不是。MellowPhil 区分“当前证据不足”和“原则性模态排除”。它追问的是排除论证的证明负担。
  • O3语言和音乐是生命会不会太诗化？
    回应：该说法应被理解为哲学-现象学命题：语言与音乐作为生成、组织、召唤与节律化经验的生命形式，而不是生物学物种判断。
  • O4哲学和科学同河会不会混淆边界？
    回应：不是混同，而是互补：哲学负责概念审计，科学负责经验约束、可证伪性与修正压力。
